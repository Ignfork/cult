FAQ

Questions from the uninitiated

Everything about founding a cult, tithes, devotion, the mark and sects.

The basics
What is CULT?

A launchpad for cult coins on pump.fun. Every coin founded here sends 100% of its pump.fun creator fees to the people who hold it, every 3 minutes, in SOL. Nothing is kept by the platform.

Why a cult and not a community?

Cults are the strongest force in crypto for pushing a coin and keeping it alive. CULT pays people to act like one: hold longer, wear the coin as their picture, and pull others in.

Is a cult a normal pump.fun coin?

Yes. Every cult is a standard pump.fun launch: 1 billion supply, 6 decimals, a fair launch on the bonding curve, no presale, no team tokens, and automatic graduation to PumpSwap. Trading happens on pump.fun.

Does the platform take a fee?

No. There is no launch fee and no cut of the fees. The only costs are Solana network fees and pump.fun's standard creation cost, plus your optional first buy.

Fees & tithes
Where do the fees go?

At launch, the coin's pump.fun fee sharing is set to 100% for the CULT payout wallet and locked. Every 3 minutes the payout desk collects those fees and tithes them in SOL to every wallet holding at least 0.2 SOL worth of the coin.

Can the founder change where fees go later?

No. Fee sharing is locked at launch. Nobody can change it afterwards, not the founder and not the operators.

How much do I need to hold to get paid?

At least 0.2 SOL worth of the cult's coin, checked at each 3-minute payout.

How is my share worked out?

share = amount held × seconds held × devotion × mark bonus. Holding more, holding longer and wearing the coin as your picture all raise your share. See how it works.

How can I check a payout really happened?

Every launch, fee collection and tithe is written to the public ledger with its Solana transaction, and the whole ledger can be downloaded.

Devotion & the mark
What is devotion?

A multiplier from 1x to 4x that grows with how long you've held: about 1.15x after a day, 2.18x after 10 days, 3.33x after 30 and 3.98x after 100.

What happens to devotion if I buy or sell?

Buying more mixes new coins into your bag, so its age drops in proportion. Selling part cuts the age by the part you sold. Selling everything starts you again at 1x.

What is bearing the mark?

Wearing the cult as your profile picture: either the cult's own picture or any picture matching the look written at launch. pump.fun counts 1.25x, X counts 1.25x once you sign in with X and link your wallet, and both together count 1.5x.

How often is the mark checked?

Every 30 minutes. The bonus ends when the picture comes off.

Where do I get a picture to wear?

Every cult page has a prompt builder: pick something to add, copy the prompt, and give it to any image tool with the cult's picture. Add the result to the cult's gallery and anyone wearing it counts.

Sects
What is a sect?

A child coin of an existing cult, paired with its parent on pump.fun, so every buy of the sect buys the parent first.

How are sect holders rewarded?

A sect carries a 0.95% creator fee paid in the parent's coin. It goes to the sect's holders unsold, as a reward for pumping the parent. Each holder decides whether to keep or sell it.

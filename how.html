How it works

Coins grow when cults do

CULT is a launchpad for cult coins. Every fee a coin earns goes to the faithful who hold it.

01

Found

You launch from your own wallet. The launch sets the coin's pump.fun fee sharing to 100 percent for the CULT payout wallet and locks it, so it can never be changed.

The cult is listed with its own picture on the front page and in Cults. The three biggest sit on the altar.

02

Every fee to the faithful

Every 3 minutes the payout desk collects the coin's creator fees and tithes them in SOL to every wallet holding at least 0.2 SOL worth of the coin. Nothing is kept by the platform. The only cost is the Solana network fee of each transaction.

Each holder's share is weighted by three things:

share = amount held × seconds held × devotion × mark bonus
03

Devotion, up to 4x

A new follower counts 1x. The longer you hold, the more you count. The climb is fastest in the first weeks and gentler every day after, towards 4x. By day 100 the line is almost flat.

The age belongs to your coins. Buying more mixes new coins into your bag, so the age drops in proportion. Selling part of your bag cuts the age by the part you sold. Selling everything starts you again from 1x.

devotion = 1 + 3 × (1 − e^(−days held / 20)) 1 day 1.15x 10 days 2.18x 30 days 3.33x 100 days 3.98x
04

Bear the mark

Followers who wear the cult as their profile picture are the ones working its bags. They earn more.

The mark counts two ways: the cult's own picture, or a picture that follows the cult's look, written at launch. If the look is "any animal wearing a black hood", a raven in a black hood on purple counts just like the original.

Your pump.fun profile picture counts 1.25x. Your X profile picture counts 1.25x once you sign in with X and link your wallet. Both together count 1.5x. Every holder is checked again every 30 minutes, and the bonus ends when the picture comes off.

05

Sects

Anyone can start a sect of a cult. A sect is paired with its parent on pump.fun, so every buy of the sect buys the parent first. The sect is listed on its parent's page.

A sect carries its own 0.95 percent creator fee, paid in the parent's coin. All of it goes to the sect's holders in the parent's coin, unsold, as their reward for pumping the parent. Each holder decides whether to keep it or sell it.

The only tokens sold are the few that pay the Solana network fees, and the rent of a new token account for a holder who has none yet, taken from that holder's own share.

06

Every tithe on the ledger

Every collection and every tithe is written to a public ledger with its Solana transaction. Open the ledger.
